# Expansion Backlog — Implement AFTER Core Works

## More uses for water
1. **Bamboo watermill**: kinetic wheel drives rice-husking / milling animation; different output than turbine.
2. **Rooftop harvesting**: tin roofs collect rain into a dedicated cistern; divert between household plant watering and treatment.
3. **Underground recharge**: sink basins absorb runoff, reviving a dry well later.
4. **Small freshwater ferry**: fill a canal route so tiny boat starts traveling between banks.
5. **Floating garden (ধাপ)**: plant vegetables on floating beds that rise with the waterline; rooted in actual Bangladeshi farming.
6. **Lotus nursery**: shallow pond areas bloom seasonally, attract butterflies/frogs.
7. **Rain-powered musical sculpture**: droplets strike bamboo chimes to create procedurally musical sequences.
8. **Aqueduct and siphon puzzle**: redirect water around obstacles (hydraulics simplified).
9. **Paddy–fish integrated farming**: shared water body supports crops and fish when managed carefully.
10. **Flood diversion gardens**: route excess water through wetlands to reduce overflow into homes.
11. **Water delivery pipes**: separate open raw canals from sealed potable-water distribution.
12. **Water-powered irrigation pump tradeoff**: use turbine output to lift water; intentionally less energy recovered than spent.
13. **Fish hatchery**: separate baby fish area with shallow, oxygenated clean-enough habitat.
14. **Duck pond**: noncompetitive tiny animations responding to pond health.
15. **Dry-season stewardship**: stored monsoon water matters when rain stops temporarily.
16. **Silt deposits / delta shaping**: water slowly creates new land and fertile banks (far future).
17. **Seasonal boat lanterns**: calm evening event rewards thriving waterways.
18. **Rainbows, waterfall, fireflies**: purely aesthetic responses to weather/flow/time.

## Longer-term modules
World seed gallery (offline), import/export and share codes, alternate Bangladesh biomes (haor, coastal plain, village char), ethical educational facts, selective cloud sync, co-op collaborative designs only if backend is warranted. No multiplayer in MVP.

## Guardrail
Do not build these while core flow is broken. New systems must have an input, a player action, an output, a visible result, and tests. Reject purely decorative menu entries masquerading as mechanics.
